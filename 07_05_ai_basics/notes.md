# AI terms, properly explained
Large Language Model - ex: gemini, chatgpt, claude etc.. There is no 'understnding module'. It ia an advanced pattern recognition system on steroids. if you type 'thank' then it continue with 'you' not because they understand gratitude. just becaue this is the most probable next word
Tokens - puzzle pieces of words, not whole words. currency of ai. and word: unbelievable tokens: unbeliev, able and cat will be cat
vector (Embeddings) - a GPS cordinate for meaning. Numerical representation of a word, that captures its meaning as a location in space
Context window - Everything a mdoel can see at once (msg, reply, docs shared) has a hard limit. Once that limit is hit, the oldest content quietly falls off to make room for new content.
LLM or AI calls are stateless
Parameters - millions of tiny knobs, none meaningfull alone. Internal values tuned during training, the model's actual settings. Capability emerges from the combined position of billions of them.
